# Cybersecurity Glossary

## Authentication

The process of verifying who a user or system is.

## Authorization

The process of determining what an authenticated user or system is allowed to do.

## Confidentiality

Protecting information from unauthorized access.

## Integrity

Protecting information from unauthorized alteration.

## Availability

Ensuring that systems and information remain accessible when needed.

## Vulnerability

A weakness that could be exploited.

## Threat

A potential cause of harm to a system, organization, or person.

## Risk

The potential impact and likelihood associated with a threat exploiting a vulnerability.

## Incident

A security-related event that may compromise confidentiality, integrity, or availability.
